PRIVACY & DATA

How NYMIC uses your data

To help you understand your relationship with nicotine, NYMIC reads and processes the body data below. You can withdraw consent or delete your data anytime in Settings.
Data we read
Heart rate
From your wearable device, used to learn your personal baseline and assess your body state.
Blood oxygen (SpO₂)
From your wearable device, used to give a gentle reminder when SpO₂ runs low.
Usage records
Your usage count and power-adjustment history, used to build your personal data profile.
How your data is protected
Purpose-limited · Data is used only for the purposes above, nothing else.
Never sold or shared · We never sell or share your body data with third parties.
You stay in control · Export, delete your data, or withdraw consent anytime in Settings.
